# COR011 / LCR model basis

BankSim maps the regulatory Liquidity Coverage Ratio to the PRA COR011 reporting structure effective from 1 January 2022: C72 liquid assets, C73 outflows, C74 inflows and C76 calculations.

## Architecture

The regulatory LCR is modelled as its own 30-day liquidity calculation rather than as a ratio derived directly from balance-sheet tags.

The implementation has four layers:

1. `src/domain/lcr.ts` defines the regulatory LCR model. An `LcrModel` consists of eligible liquid-asset lines, 30-day outflow lines, 30-day inflow lines and optional C76 HQLA adjustments. These are regulatory cash-flow / liquidity-model objects, not balance-sheet positions.
2. `src/engine/lcrEngine.ts` is the pure calculation engine. It knows how to calculate HQLA composition limits, C76 secured-transaction adjustments, the fully exempt / 90% / 75% inflow populations, net liquidity outflow and the final LCR. It has no dependency on `BankState`, product types, balance-sheet sides, funding ladders, loan cohorts or hedge accounting.
3. `src/engine/cor011.ts` is the BankSim adapter. It translates the richer simulation state into an `LcrModel`: contractual funding cash flows, loan repayments, undrawn commitments, derivative netting-set cash flows, eligible HQLA and secured-funding collateral effects.
4. `src/engine/metrics.ts` uses the result of that engine as the authoritative regulatory `hqla` and `lcr` metrics. The LCR dashboard consumes the same COR011 calculation, so there is only one regulatory calculation path.

This boundary is intended to make future LCR extensions additive. For example:

- a new Level 2A or Level 2B asset adds an eligible C72 contribution; the central composition-cap logic does not change;
- a second derivative counterparty or netting set adds another C73/C74 cash-flow line; the engine does not change;
- a collateral swap or secured transaction adds C76 HQLA adjustments; the engine does not need product-specific logic;
- a new 90%-cap or fully exempt inflow population specifies its inflow cap class; the C76 formula does not change.

The adapter may currently derive many of these inputs from balance-sheet and contractual-state data, but that is an integration detail. The LCR engine itself operates on the regulatory 30-day model, not on the balance sheet.

## Current product assumptions

- Cash and reserves are treated as withdrawable central-bank reserves (C72 1.1.1.2).
- UK gilts are treated as Level 1 central-government assets (C72 1.1.1.4).
- `insuredRetailDepositShare` is used as a BankSim proxy for the share of retail deposits meeting the full stable-retail-deposit conditions. Deposit-guarantee coverage alone is not sufficient under the regulatory definition.
- SME / Business Operating Deposits are assumed to meet the operational-deposit conditions and map to C73 1.1.2.3.
- Other Business Deposits are assumed not to be DGS-covered and map to C73 1.1.4.3.2.
- Senior and Tier 2 debt maturing within 30 days map to C73 1.1.8.2.
- Bank of England funding is secured by Level 1 UK gilts and maps to C73 1.2.1.1, with a 0% outflow factor. Short-term secured-funding unwind amounts are represented as C76 HQLA adjustments.
- Mortgage and consumer undrawn credit facilities are treated as retail facilities (C73 1.1.6.1.1, 5%). Corporate facilities map to C73 1.1.6.1.2 (10%).
- Performing loan interest due over the next month maps to C74 1.1.1.1 at 100%. Principal from retail customers maps to C74 1.1.1.2.1 at 50%; principal from non-financial corporate customers maps to C74 1.1.1.2.2 at 50%.
- Derivative cash flows are currently represented as a single bilateral netting set because BankSim does not yet model derivative counterparties separately. A net outflow maps to C73 1.1.5.5; a net inflow maps to C74 1.1.9. The standalone engine already supports multiple independent lines/netting sets.
- All inflows currently generated by BankSim fall in the 75% cap class. The standalone engine implements the 75%, 90% and fully exempt inflow classes separately.
- C75 collateral swaps and liquid-asset classes other than current Level 1 reserves/gilts are not yet generated by the current BankSim adapter. The standalone engine supports the required Level 2 composition and generic C76 adjustment mechanics so those features can be added without changing the core calculation.

## Regulatory versus management liquidity

`RiskMetrics.lcr` and `RiskMetrics.hqla` are regulatory metrics produced by the dedicated COR011/LCR engine.

`managementLcr` remains a separate behavioural game metric. It stresses deposit runoff and inflow realisation using BankSim's confidence and macro assumptions and is intentionally not presented as the regulatory COR011 return.
